Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Orivo Health Inc.  ·  hello@orivohealth.com

Accessibility is core to our mission. Orivo is built on the belief that healthcare communication should reach everyone — including people using assistive technology, people with disabilities, and people on older or simpler devices. We are committed to making this site and any future services as accessible as possible.

Our target standard

Orivo Health aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. This is the standard referenced by the U.S. Department of Justice for public-facing digital services and represents our ongoing accessibility target for this prototype site and all future products.

Current status

This site is a prototype under active development. We are working toward WCAG 2.2 AA conformance. Our current implementation includes:

🔍
Semantic structure
Heading hierarchy, landmark regions, and meaningful page structure
⌨️
Keyboard navigation
Core navigation and interactive elements are keyboard-accessible
🌐
Language
Page language is declared; multilingual content is in scope for future work
📱
Responsive design
Site is designed to reflow at mobile viewport sizes without horizontal scroll
🎨
Color contrast
Primary text and interactive elements target WCAG AA contrast ratios
✋
Motion
Animated elements are decorative; we are evaluating prefers-reduced-motion support

Known limitations

As a prototype site under active development, we are aware of the following limitations we are working to address:

  • Some interactive demo elements (animated SMS sequences, pipeline diagrams) may not be fully described for screen reader users. We are adding ARIA labels and descriptive text to these elements.
  • Some decorative SVG elements may not be hidden from assistive technology. We are auditing these.
  • Focus indicators on some custom interactive elements may not meet WCAG 2.2 AA requirements. We are reviewing and improving these.
  • Alternative text for complex diagrams is in progress.

We are conducting ongoing accessibility testing as the site evolves and will update this statement as improvements are made.

Future product accessibility commitments

Orivo's core product is an SMS-first communication workflow — which is itself an accessibility-first design decision. SMS reaches patients who cannot use apps, who have older devices, or who have limited digital literacy. Our product accessibility commitments for future pilot services include:

  • Plain language at a 6th–8th grade reading level for all patient-facing communications
  • Multilingual support as a first-class requirement, not an afterthought
  • Compatibility with screen readers and assistive technology in any future web-based provider tools
  • Voice channel as a future modality for patients who cannot use SMS
  • WCAG 2.2 AA as the minimum target for any provider-facing dashboard or portal
