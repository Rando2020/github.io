Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Orivo Health exists to lower barriers to care, so accessibility is part of the mission, not a compliance afterthought. We want everyone, including people using assistive technology, to be able to use this website.

Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. As a small, early-stage company we test on an ongoing basis and fix issues as we find them; conformance is a continuing effort rather than a one-time certification.

What we do

  • Semantic HTML headings and landmarks for screen reader navigation
  • Visible keyboard focus on links and controls
  • Color contrast checked against AA thresholds on text and buttons
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and diagrams
  • No content that flashes, and respect for reduced-motion preferences
  • Plain-language copy written for a broad reading level

Known limitations

Some older prototype and demonstration pages may not yet fully meet our standard. We are reviewing and updating them, and we prioritize fixes that affect core pages and navigation.

Tell us if something isn't working

If any part of this site is difficult for you to use, we genuinely want to know. Email josiah.parve@orivohealth.com with the page address and a description of the problem. We aim to respond within five business days.
