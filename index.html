How Orivo works

Listen. Translate. Connect.

Three steps. Any phone. Any language. The patient finally has something in their corner — and the care team has the context to act.

💬
Patient speaks naturally
Via SMS, voice, or app — no forms, no portals, no jargon. Symptoms, questions, barriers, in their own words. Orivo loads their specific insurance benefits automatically.
🧠
Aevo translates & enriches
Extracts symptoms, flags urgency, checks medication history, identifies ADEs, surfaces SDoH barriers, maps the patient to every resource their insurance provides — in under 4 seconds.
🤝
Patient is heard. Team can act.
The patient gets clear plain-language responses with specific next steps. The care team gets a structured clinical brief. Both sides feel supported, not overwhelmed.

Why we exist

Built from the front lines.

I stood at the pharmacy counter in underserved communities and watched patients arrive with 20 medications they couldn't name — leaving with instructions they didn't understand, feeling like a problem to be managed, not a person to be heard.

I've seen what no healthcare access looks like — from the counter of a CVS in an underserved neighborhood to clinics in Africa with Hope Without Borders. The gap isn't clinical. It's communication.

"Providers had Epic. Payers had data platforms. Pharmacies had dispensing systems. Nobody had built anything for the patient. So we did."
