How Orivo works

Listen. Translate. Connect.

Orivo starts with natural patient language and turns it into structured context for human review.

01
Patient speaks naturally
Symptoms, confusion, affordability issues, transportation barriers, and adherence concerns arrive via SMS-first workflows.
02
Aevo extracts context
Patient language is translated into medication concern, barrier type, urgency tier, and reviewable summary.
03
Vera validates boundaries
Safety, evidence, benefit, and routing checks keep the product grounded and human-in-the-loop.
04
The right team acts
Pharmacy, provider, payer, or care team receives the view they need without erasing the patient’s words.
