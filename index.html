The problem

Most medication questions never get answered.

Patients stop taking medications because they're confused, scared, or can't afford them — and there's no easy way to say so. The gap isn't clinical. It's communication.

~50%

How it works

Four steps. No app required.

Orivo turns a patient's plain-language text into a structured brief that a pharmacist can act on — without replacing clinical judgment.

01
Patient texts naturally
Any phone. Any language. No app to download. The patient describes what they're experiencing in their own words.
02
Context is extracted
Orivo identifies the concern, barrier type, and urgency level — and preserves the patient's original words alongside it.
03
Safety boundaries checked
Nothing clinical is generated by AI. Urgent signals are flagged immediately. The system stays in its lane.
04
Pharmacist sees a brief
The care team gets a structured, reviewable summary — not a wall of raw text — with the context to respond quickly and confidently.
Why it works

Built around what already works in healthcare.

Orivo doesn't try to replace clinical care. It removes the communication gap that exists before care even starts.

💬
SMS is universal
Every phone can receive a text. No app install, no account, no learning curve. Patients engage because the barrier to start is zero.
👁
Human-in-the-loop
AI extracts and structures. A real person — pharmacist, care coordinator, or provider — always makes the decision. No autonomous clinical actions.
📋
Context, not just a message
The pharmacist doesn't just get a forwarded text. They get a structured brief: what the patient said, what it might mean, and what needs a response.
