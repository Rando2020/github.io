Competitive moat
Five layers of defensibility
that compound over time.
A moat is not a single feature a competitor can copy. It is the accumulation of relationships, data, trust, and infrastructure that makes switching painful and replication impossible. Orivo is building all five layers simultaneously from day one.
🤝
Patient trust
Patients who feel heard don't switch. Trust built through consistent, empathetic, accurate responses compounds into long-term engagement that no competitor can purchase.
📊
Vera data layer
Conversational health data is not available anywhere else. Every interaction builds a dataset that improves Aevo's accuracy - and eventually becomes Orivo Intel, a product no competitor can replicate without years of patient relationships.
🏥
Health system contracts
Health system IT procurement is 12-24 months. Once integrated, switching costs are enormous. The first three pilots lock in the contract template, the EHR integrations, and the clinical workflow - creating a flywheel for expansion.
⚡
Insurance integrations
Loading a patient's specific benefit structure - NEMT coverage, formulary, PAP eligibility - requires bilateral data relationships with payers. These take time to build and create exclusivity. Each payer relationship is a distribution channel.
🔐
Compliance infrastructure
SOC 2, HIPAA readiness review, BAA framework, pen test history, and HITRUST are not bought - they are built over years of operation. A new entrant can't shortcut this. Orivo's compliance infrastructure is itself a competitive barrier.

Risks and mitigations - honest answers
Regulatory risk
HIPAA alignment is complex and slow
Health data regulation requires significant upfront investment and ongoing attention. A breach or compliance failure could be fatal at seed stage.
Mitigation: 25% of seed allocated to compliance. Vanta from day one. Healthcare attorney on retainer. SOC 2 Type I before first pilot signs. HIPAA baked into L0-L8 stack architecture.
AI liability risk
What if Aevo gets something wrong?
An AI-generated clinical suggestion that leads to patient harm is the existential risk for any health AI company.
Mitigation: Vera validates every output. Aevo is a CDS tool, not a diagnostic system. Human review layer in H1. Hover-to-original preserves patient voice. BAA language defines liability boundaries clearly.
Market risk
Health system sales cycles are long
Enterprise health system contracts can take 18-24 months from first conversation to signed agreement.
Mitigation: H1 pilots target independent pharmacies - 30-90 day sales cycles, personal relationships, no IT procurement. Health system conversations are H2, funded by pilot ARR.
Technology risk
LLMs make mistakes on clinical content
Hallucination in a clinical context is not just an inconvenience - it is a patient safety issue.
Mitigation: RAG-grounded outputs. Vera's six-check Verafy system. Confidence scoring with human review below threshold. Output constraints baked into every Aevo system prompt. First Databank as ground truth.
