Privacy Notice

Last updated: June 10, 2026

The short version: this website is informational. It does not ask for, and you should not submit, any medical information, medication lists, insurance details, or other health information through this site.

Who we are

Orivo Health Inc. ("Orivo," "we," "us") is a Delaware corporation developing an SMS-first medication communication platform, currently in the pilot stage. This notice describes how we handle information collected through this public website, orivohealth.com.

What this site collects

  • Contact information you choose to send us. If you email us or request a meeting, we receive your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider may log standard information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and operational purposes.

This site does not include forms that request health information, and we ask that you do not include health information in emails to us.

What this site does not collect

This public website does not collect protected health information (PHI), medication or prescription details, symptoms, insurance information, or dates of birth. Any future patient-facing services involving health information will operate on separate, secured systems with their own privacy terms presented before use.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries about pilots, partnerships, or the company
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use information from this site for third-party advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run this site and our email (for example, hosting and email providers), or where required by law.

Retention

We keep correspondence as long as needed for the purpose it was sent and for reasonable business record-keeping, then delete it.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing josiah.parve@orivohealth.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law.

Changes

We will update this notice as the company and its services evolve, and we will change the date above when we do.
