Privacy Notice

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Orivo Health Inc.  ·  hello@orivohealth.com

Plain-language summary: This is a static prototype marketing website. We do not collect patient health information, medications, symptoms, or insurance details through this site. If you reached this page looking for a patient portal or health service, please contact your pharmacist or care team directly.

What this site is

Orivohealth.com is a public prototype and marketing website for Orivo Health Inc., a pilot-stage company building an SMS-first medication communication workflow. This site contains informational content, investor materials, and interactive demos. It is not a patient portal, a health app, a telehealth service, or a covered entity under HIPAA.

This site does not accept, transmit, or store protected health information (PHI). No patient enrollment, medication uploads, symptom collection, or insurance verification takes place through this website.

Information we may collect

When you visit this site, we may collect:

  • Contact information — if you voluntarily submit your name or email address through a contact form or demo request, we use that only to respond to you.
  • Basic usage data — if analytics are active, we may collect anonymized page-view counts, referral sources, and approximate geographic region (country/state level). We do not use advertising trackers or pixels on this site.
  • Cookies — this site may use minimal session cookies for basic site functionality. We do not use tracking cookies, behavioral advertising cookies, or third-party marketing pixels.

We do not collect: health conditions, medications, symptoms, diagnoses, insurance information, Social Security numbers, date of birth, or any other sensitive personal data through this public site.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry or demo request
  • To understand how this prototype site is used so we can improve it
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties for marketing

Third-party services

This site may use the following third-party services:

  • Cal.com — for scheduling demo calls. If you book a call, Cal.com's own privacy policy applies to information you provide during scheduling.
  • GitHub Pages — this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's privacy policy governs their infrastructure-level data handling.
  • Plausible Analytics — if active, we use Plausible, a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data.

Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry. We do not maintain long-term marketing lists from this prototype site.

Your rights

You may request that we delete any contact information you submitted through this site. To do so, email hello@orivohealth.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Future services

If Orivo Health launches secure patient-facing services in the future, those services will operate under separate privacy agreements, business associate agreements (BAAs), and data handling frameworks appropriate to those workflows. They will not be hosted on this static GitHub Pages site.
