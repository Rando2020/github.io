What Aevo will do
Listen to patient messages and ask clarifying questions in plain language
Extract symptoms, barriers, and concerns - with confidence scores on every item
Load the patient's specific insurance benefits and surface what exists for them
Flag possible ADEs passively and notify the verified care team
Notify authenticated pharmacists and physicians through verified, HIPAA-conscious channels
Route to the right person - pharmacist, physician, or care coordinator - based on content
Surface 911 and crisis resources immediately when urgency score hits 5
Preserve the original patient voice verbatim - always accessible on hover
Send outbound voice calls to pharmacy lines when SMS is not sufficient
Designed for SMS and voice access across basic phones and smartphones
🚫
What Aevo will never do
Diagnose a medical condition - Aevo informs, flags, and defers. Never diagnoses.
Tell a patient to stop taking a medication - this requires a licensed provider
Tell a patient "this is not serious" - Aevo never minimizes clinical concern
Send PHI to an unverified number - every recipient is authenticated before any message is sent
Send a notification to anyone not on the patient's verified care team
Guess when confidence is below threshold - low-confidence outputs are flagged, not delivered
Recommend specific treatments or medications unprompted
Store PHI in any non-encrypted, non-HIPAA-eligible system
Override or replace the clinical judgment of a licensed provider
Deliver any output that has not been Verafy'd by Vera first

HIPAA-conscious notification routing - who gets what
Role verification · Consent check · Channel selection · PHI minimization
Pending
1
Look up patient's registered care team - only people on this list can receive any notification. Set at enrollment. Updated only by patient or admin.
2
Verify pharmacist identity - Twilio Lookup confirms the destination number belongs to the registered pharmacy. Cross-checked against NPI registry. BAA must be on file.
3
Verify physician identity - physician's notification number verified against their NPI and EHR provider record. Role-tagged in patient record as prescribing physician.
4
PHI minimization applied - notification contains only what the recipient needs for this specific clinical action. No full record sent. No unnecessary PHI transmitted.
5
Channel selected by tier and role - pharmacist gets SMS + optional voice. Physician gets secure in-basket + SMS. Emergency gets all channels simultaneously.
⚖️  HIPAA fence - hard rules
A notification is NEVER sent to a number that has not been pre-verified and role-tagged in the patient's care team record
The pharmacist's number is confirmed against NPI + BAA on file before every single send - not just at enrollment
If identity cannot be verified, notification is held and flagged for human review - it is never sent speculatively
All notifications are logged immutably: who was notified, what was sent, what channel, what time - Vera's audit trail

What Vera will do
Validate every Aevo output for semantic consistency, benefit accuracy, and hallucination risk
Build the immutable audit trail - every message, translation, notification, and action timestamped and signed
Monitor AI drift weekly against a golden test set of 500+ validated clinical conversations
Detect when Aevo's translation quality degrades before it reaches a patient
Capture and structure SDoH signals from conversations into ICD-10 Z-codes
Build the de-identified population health dataset that powers Orivo Intel
Flag inbound messages that show coaching signatures or fraud patterns
Score every output on six validation dimensions before delivery
Feed provider correction data back into Aevo's improvement pipeline
Generate HIPAA-aligned audit exports for legal, compliance, and accreditation
🚫
What Vera will never do
Share identifiable patient data with any payer, health system, or third party
Allow an Aevo output to reach a patient without passing all six Verafy checks
Let PHI enter the Snowflake analytics warehouse - de-identification is enforced at pipeline boundary
Override an Aevo output silently - all Vera interventions are logged and flagged to the review team
Approve an output where confidence falls below the defined threshold
Delete or modify any item in the audit trail - the log is append-only and cryptographically signed
Allow the AI drift score to exceed threshold without triggering human review and retraining
Approve a notification to an unverified recipient - identity check is required every time
Suppress a fraud or coaching signal - all flags surface to the compliance team automatically
Be used as the sole decision-maker - Vera validates and flags. Humans decide.
