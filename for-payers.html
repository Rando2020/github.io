Why payers care

The plan already pays for many resources. Members still struggle to find and use them.

The payer problem is not only whether benefits exist. It is whether members can understand them at the exact moment they are deciding whether to fill a medication, attend a visit, call a nurse line, request transportation, or abandon the process entirely.

What to measure in pilot

Medication adherence risk

Member says they stopped, skipped, cannot afford, forgot, or felt side effects.

Aevo captures the barrier, checks plan and pharmacy context, gives safe education, and routes medication concerns to pharmacist or care team when required.

Transportation and missed visits

Member cannot attend a visit because of ride, caregiver, mobility, or distance barriers.

Orivo identifies transportation intent, checks plan type and routing rules, and directs the member to the correct NEMT or care management path.

appointment salvageNEMT route completionmissed visit reason

Benefit confusion

Member does not know if telehealth, home delivery, OTC, pharmacy support, or ride support exists.

Orivo translates the question, loads the member's configured benefit pathways, and explains the next step in plain language.

benefit activationself-service completioncall deflection hypothesis

Medication safety concern

Member mentions dizziness, chest tightness, swelling, bleeding, confusion, or other possible adverse effects.

Orivo uses safety boundaries, advises urgent resources when needed, and sends a structured brief for clinical review instead of making medication decisions.

Pilot structure

A payer pilot should prove workflow lift before claiming outcomes.

We should not claim Orivo reduces readmissions, improves Stars, or cuts cost until a pilot proves it. The site now frames those as measurable hypotheses.

30 days
Configure
  • Select population: MA, D-SNP, Medicaid, or adherence cohort.
  • Load benefit pathways and escalation rules.
  • Define safety boundaries and reporting taxonomy.
60 days
Run
  • Launch SMS and voice intake for a defined cohort.
  • Route adherence, transportation, and medication safety signals.
  • Review weekly barrier and escalation reports.
90 days
Evaluate
  • Compare engagement, route completion, callbacks, and closed-loop status.
  • Assess directionality for adherence and care-gap signals.
  • Decide whether to expand, narrow, or stop.

Claims guardrail

Say: designed to support adherence operations.
Do not say: proven to improve adherence.
Say: detects transportation barriers and routes members to plan-specific next steps.
Do not say: guarantees ride completion.
Say: can generate auditable member-interaction signals for plan review.
Do not say: certified Stars improvement engine.
