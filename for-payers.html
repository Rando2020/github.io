Pilot KPIs

What a payer pilot should prove.

Do not start by promising Star Ratings lift. Start by proving Orivo can capture barriers earlier, route them safely, and generate useful de-identified patterns.

✓Member activation and response rate
✓Barrier category capture rate
✓Percent of issues routed to the right human team
✓Ride, affordability, and med confusion trend detection
✓Resolution follow-up completion
✓De-identified pattern usefulness for quality teams
Claims boundary: Orivo is prototype-stage. This page describes intended payer workflows and illustrative analytics, not validated health plan outcomes. Outcomes such as PDC lift, Star Ratings improvement, ADE reduction, or utilization reduction require pilot validation.
