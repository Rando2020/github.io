Unlocked conversation

The page scrolls. The workflow unfolds.

Patient text

I stopped taking the new blood pressure pill because it made me dizzy. I also missed my follow-up because I didn’t have a ride.

Aevo patient response

I’m sorry that happened. Dizziness after starting a new blood pressure medicine is worth reviewing with your pharmacist or care team. If symptoms feel severe, sudden, or worsening, seek urgent care now. I can also help flag that you missed your appointment because of transportation.

Vera validation layer

Vera prevents the AI from diagnosing or changing medication instructions. The system marks this as: medication concern, adherence risk, transportation barrier, and human review recommended.

Pharmacy brief

Priority: Review medication concern. Patient reports dizziness after new BP medication and self-discontinuation. Original patient words preserved. Suggested action: pharmacist callback, side-effect review, and adherence counseling.

Provider / care team brief

Care coordination note: Follow-up appointment missed due to transportation barrier. Medication discontinuation may affect blood pressure control. Suggested owner: pharmacist for medication review, care coordinator for ride benefit support.

Health plan intelligence

For the payer, this individual message can roll into de-identified pattern analytics: ride barrier, ZIP-level access issue, medication class concern, adherence risk, and benefit education opportunity.

Four stakeholder views

Same signal. Different product surfaces.

Patient

Simple next step

Plain-language response, safety boundary, and confirmation that a human is being looped in.

Pharmacy

Counseling queue

Medication concern, side-effect language, self-discontinuation, and callback priority.

Provider

Structured brief

Original patient words, medication context, urgency tier, and care coordination barrier.

Health plan

Pattern intelligence

De-identified trends by barrier type, ZIP, medication class, benefit gap, and time window.

Why this UX is better

No more trapped demo box.

The old version made the user scroll inside a phone panel. This version keeps the SMS metaphor, but uses the browser page as the scroll surface so the user can keep moving naturally.
