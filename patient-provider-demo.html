Care-team map

The bigger system: one number tied to many care relationships.

This keeps the vision ambitious without pretending it is already built. Orivo becomes the connective layer around the patient’s plan, pharmacy, provider, appointments, and future scheduling workflows.

Patient

One SMS number

Plain-language medication questions, ride barriers, appointment needs, and follow-up confirmations.

Plan

Benefit context

NEMT, telehealth, nurse line, MTM, care management, and pharmacy benefit support.

Pharmacy

Medication owner

Side effects, refill confusion, counseling, adherence barriers, and intervention documentation.

Provider

Clinical owner

Upcoming appointments, telehealth triage, structured patient-reported briefs, and care team routing.

Future

Scheduling agent

With consent and compliance, voice AI could call facilities, check availability, schedule, and text confirmation.

Claims boundary: This is a prototype vision. Insurance benefits, telehealth routing, appointment access, and voice scheduling require plan-specific contracts, eligibility checks, consent, BAAs, security review, integration work, and clinical oversight before production use.
