System concept

Orivo becomes the connective layer around the patient.

The patient does not need to know which portal, pharmacy app, benefits line, or clinic number to use. Orivo knows the care-team graph and routes the concern to the right place.

Unlocked care-team flow

The workflow unfolds as the patient scrolls.

Patient text

I stopped taking the new blood pressure pill because it made me dizzy, and I missed my follow-up because I didn’t have a ride. Can I just talk to someone online?

Aevo patient response

I’m sorry that happened. Dizziness after starting a new blood pressure medicine should be reviewed by a pharmacist or care team. If symptoms feel severe, sudden, or worsening, seek urgent care now. I can also check whether your plan offers telehealth or ride support.

Vera benefit and safety check

Vera checks plan type, available support routes, safety boundaries, and escalation rules. It does not let the AI change medication instructions or diagnose. It marks this as medication concern, adherence risk, transportation barrier, and telehealth routing candidate.

Pharmacy review

Priority: medication review needed. Patient reports dizziness after new BP medication and self-discontinuation. Pharmacist callback recommended before generic medication guidance is sent.

Provider recommendation

Telehealth recommendation: schedule a telehealth visit within 24 to 48 hours if symptoms are stable and non-urgent. Medium priority Escalate to urgent care or emergency guidance if chest pain, severe dizziness, fainting, shortness of breath, or worsening symptoms are reported.

Payer intelligence

The health plan sees de-identified patterning: ride barrier, possible medication discontinuation, telehealth demand, ZIP-level transportation friction, and benefit education opportunity. If ride-related messages rise over 90 days, the plan can review NEMT education, vendor performance, or outreach design.

Future scheduling agent

Long-term, with patient consent and approved workflows, Orivo could trigger a voice agent such as ElevenLabs to call the clinic, ask for appointment availability, schedule a telehealth visit, and text the confirmed time back to the patient.
