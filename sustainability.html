How Orivo sustains itself

Built to last.
Not built to extract.

Healthcare has enough middlemen taking a percentage of everything that moves. Orivo is designed around a simpler idea: we charge a flat, transparent fee for software that works — and our partners keep 100% of the clinical and performance dollars the platform helps them earn.

Flat, transparent fees No revenue skimming No patient inducements Pricing finalized with pilot partners
Our operating philosophy

Revenue should be a symptom of closing care gaps — not the point of them.

Orivo was founded from the pharmacy counter, watching patients abandon medications over cost, confusion, language, and transportation — and watching pharmacists lose money on the very performance measures those abandoned fills destroy. Our goal is a self-sustaining company: one where the economics stay simple enough that a pharmacy owner can understand exactly what they pay and exactly what they get.

1
We sell software, not a cut of your reimbursement. Pharmacies and health plans pay a predictable subscription. When adherence measures improve or performance penalties shrink, that value belongs entirely to the partner and the patient.
2
Fees are set at fair market value for the software delivered. We deliberately avoid percentage-of-recovery, per-referral, and per-service-rendered pricing tied to federally reimbursed care. That structure keeps our incentives aligned with patients — and keeps our partners clear of arrangements that regulators scrutinize.
3
Sustainability over scale-at-any-cost. Our internal bar is a business that covers its own operating costs on a modest number of pharmacy partnerships — so we never have to compromise the product, the data ethics, or the mission to chase growth.
Where revenue comes from

Four levers. One pricing structure.

Every lever below is charged the same way: a flat software fee, scaled to the size of the program. What changes is the value the partner unlocks.

Lever 01 · Core platform
SMS communication & barrier surfacing
The Orivo Relay subscription: two-way, plain-language medication texting with human-in-the-loop pharmacist routing. Patients ask questions, surface cost and access barriers, and get routed to a real person — on any phone, with no app.
How it's pricedPer-pharmacy monthly subscription, tiered by enrolled patients. The partner's ROI case: reduced abandoned fills, faster question resolution, and staff time returned to the counter.
Lever 02 · Performance programs
Adherence & quality-measure support
Pharmacy performance payments increasingly hinge on adherence measures like PDC. Orivo's outreach catches the refill gaps and new-start drop-offs that drag those scores down — plus preventive-care reminders such as vaccine outreach, delivered as education and scheduling help, never as patient payment or inducement.
How it's pricedAn add-on module at a flat fee. The pharmacy keeps every performance dollar. Orivo's pilots will measure the operational signals — response rates, gap closures, intervention completions — that make that value visible.
Lever 03 · Documentation
Intervention & social-need documentation
When a pharmacist resolves a barrier or intervenes on a medication, that work is usually invisible. Orivo structures it: timestamped intervention records, standards-aligned export (Pharmacist eCare Plan), and social-need barriers captured in a form partners can use to support their own SDOH documentation and coding — such as ICD-10 Z-code capture by their clinical teams.
How it's pricedA documentation module fee. Orivo does not bill codes, does not code on a partner's behalf, and takes no share of any reimbursement — we make the pharmacist's work legible and portable.
Lever 04 · Longer term
Health plan & network partnerships
Once pharmacy pilots produce real operational evidence, the same platform serves health plans and pharmacy networks reaching Medicare and Medicaid members who are unreachable by app-based tools. Aggregate, de-identified barrier intelligence helps plans understand where their members actually get stuck.
How it's pricedPer-member-per-month contracts and program licensing — negotiated after pilot evidence exists, never sold on projections we haven't earned.
Guardrails

What we deliberately don't do

A sustainable healthcare business is defined as much by the revenue it refuses as the revenue it accepts.

✕ No percentage of reimbursement
We don't take a cut of performance payments, MTM fees, vaccine revenue, or recovered dollars. Percentage arrangements tied to federal healthcare program funds invite regulatory risk and misalign incentives.
✕ No patient inducements
Reminders and education, yes. Paying or rewarding patients to receive care, no. Outreach is designed to inform and remove barriers — never to steer utilization for anyone's financial benefit.
✕ No selling patient data
Patient-level information is never sold. Any future intelligence products use aggregate, de-identified insights, shared under contracts partners approve.
✕ No invented outcomes
We're pilot-stage and we say so. Adherence, cost, and outcome claims appear on this site only after real pilots measure them.
Who we serve first

Independents first. Networks and chains as we prove it.

Now · Independent & community pharmacies

The first partners

Independent pharmacies sit closest to the moments Orivo is built for — the fill that's late, the new start that's confusing, the patient who quietly stops picking up. Nearly 36% of retail pharmacies are independent, and Medicare and Medicaid account for the majority of their prescriptions.

Model: direct, affordable subscriptions with fast setup — a roster export and SMS consent, not a year-long integration project. Pricing is being finalized with our first pilot partners.

Later · Networks, chains & health plans

The scale path

Community pharmacy networks offer a single-agreement path to thousands of locations once a workflow proves out. Regional chains and health plans follow — with enterprise licensing, deeper integration, and the security review that scale requires.

Model: enterprise and per-member-per-month contracts, entered only when pilot evidence supports them.
