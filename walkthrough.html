Core script

Orivo starts where patients actually are: SMS. The patient does not need an app, portal, password, or clinical vocabulary. Aevo listens for medication confusion, side effects, access barriers, and adherence risks. The system does not diagnose or prescribe. It translates patient language into reviewable clinical context. Vera checks the output, preserves the original patient message, and logs the action. The pharmacist or care team receives the brief, reviews it, and sends a human-approved response. Orivo translates that response back into plain language. The first pilot measures response rate, medication concerns captured, pharmacist escalations, barriers identified, time to review, and patient-reported understanding.
