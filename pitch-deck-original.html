The Medicare/Medicaid crisis
The highest-risk patients are managing 10-25+ medications across multiple prescribers who don't talk to each other. They don't know what they're taking, why, or what their insurance actually covers. ADEs in this population cost CMS $3.5B annually in preventable spending.
This isn't a clinical problem. It's a communication problem. AI can solve it.
How Orivo works
Listen. Translate. Connect.
Three steps. SMS-first access. Plain-language support. Care-team context when it matters.
💬
Patient speaks naturally
Via SMS or the Orivo app. No forms, no portals, no jargon. Symptoms, questions, concerns, barriers - in their own words. We ask about their insurance and load what's available to them specifically.
🧠
Aevo translates, enriches & connects
Extracts symptoms, flags urgency, checks medication history, identifies ADEs, surfaces SDoH barriers, and maps the patient to every resource their insurance and community provides - in under 4 seconds. Vera validates every output.
🤝
Patient is heard. Team can act.
The patient receives clear, plain-language responses with specific next steps. The care team receives a Verafy'd clinical brief. Both sides feel supported. The original patient voice is always preserved and accessible.
