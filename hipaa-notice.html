PHI & Clinical Safety Notice

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Orivo Health Inc.  ·  hello@orivohealth.com

⚠ Medical emergency? If you or someone near you is experiencing a medical emergency — including chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe allergic reaction, loss of consciousness, or thoughts of self-harm — call 911 immediately or go to your nearest emergency room. Do not use this website in an emergency.

What this notice covers

This notice explains how Orivo Health approaches protected health information (PHI), clinical safety, and the limits of what this prototype website can and cannot do. It is not a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices — Orivo Health is not currently a covered entity under HIPAA and is not required to publish one.

This notice is intended for transparency with patients, partners, and the public about what this site is, what it is not, and how any future secure services will be handled.

This public website does not handle PHI. This static prototype site does not accept, transmit, process, or store protected health information. No patient enrollment, medication data, symptom information, insurance identifiers, or clinical data of any kind is collected through orivohealth.com.

Orivo's approach to PHI in future services

When Orivo launches secure pilot services, PHI will be handled under a framework separate from this static website. That framework will include:

  • Explicit, documented patient consent before any SMS workflow begins
  • Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with covered entity partners before any PHI workflow is live
  • HIPAA-eligible cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest
  • Audit logging of every message event, model version, human review action, and delivery status
  • Hard escalation rules that route emergency or high-risk content to a human immediately
  • No AI-generated final responses to high-risk symptom reports without human review
  • Opt-out and consent revocation honored immediately per FCC requirements

Secure patient-facing services will not be hosted on this GitHub Pages site. They will operate on separate, HIPAA-eligible infrastructure under appropriate legal and compliance frameworks.

AI safety guardrails — design intent

Orivo is designing its AI layer with explicit limits. The following is the intended design for pilot deployments — these guardrails are not yet active on any live service:

  • AI will not diagnose. The system is designed to classify and route patient messages, not to provide clinical diagnoses.
  • AI will not recommend changing medication. The system is not designed to advise patients to start, stop, or adjust doses.
  • AI will not provide false reassurance for urgent symptoms. High-risk symptom categories — including chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe allergic reactions, and neurological symptoms — will receive emergency guidance first and immediate human escalation, never a reassuring automated response.
  • A pharmacist or care team member reviews high-risk outputs before any response is sent to a patient.
  • Original patient language is always preserved in the audit trail and provider review queue.

Human in the loop. Orivo's design intent is that AI classifies and drafts — humans review and send. This is especially true for anything involving symptom concerns, medication safety, or high-risk escalation categories. No automated final response will be sent for high-risk content.

Demo scenarios involving symptom responses are illustrative only and should not be interpreted as clinical guidance.

Nothing on this website — including any demo, workflow description, or illustrative content — constitutes medical advice, clinical guidance, or a recommendation to take or change any medication. Always consult a licensed pharmacist, physician, or other qualified healthcare provider about your medications and health decisions.
