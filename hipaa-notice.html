Protected Health Information & Clinical Safety

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Orivo Health is a pilot-stage technology company. This page explains, in plain language, how we approach protected health information (PHI) and clinical safety, and what this public website is and is not.

Emergencies: Orivo is not an emergency service. If you think you are having a medical emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) right away. Do not wait for a text response.

This website does not handle PHI

orivohealth.com is a static, informational site. It has no patient intake, no medication uploads, no symptom collection, and no live messaging workflows. Please do not send health information through this site or by email.

How pilot deployments handle PHI

When Orivo runs pilot programs with healthcare partners, any workflow involving PHI operates on separate, secured systems — not on this website. Our design requirements for those deployments include:

  • Business associate agreements (BAAs) with healthcare partners and with vendors (such as messaging providers) before any PHI is processed
  • Express patient consent before SMS outreach, with clear opt-out honored immediately (reply STOP at any time)
  • Human-in-the-loop review: pilot messaging involving clinical content is reviewed by a pharmacist or qualified staff member before patient-facing decisions are made
  • Audit trails recording what was suggested, what a human reviewer changed, and what was sent
  • Data minimization: collecting only what a pilot needs, and retaining it only as long as the pilot and our partner agreements require

These are design requirements for secure deployments. Specific safeguards in any given pilot are defined in written agreements with that pilot partner.

About HIPAA

In pilot deployments, Orivo expects to operate as a service provider (business associate) to healthcare organizations, handling PHI only as permitted by written agreements with those organizations. A business associate is not required to publish a Notice of Privacy Practices; the healthcare organizations we serve maintain their own notices for their patients. This page is a practical statement of our approach, not a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Clinical safety principles

  • Orivo does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace your pharmacist or doctor.
  • Messages that suggest an urgent or emergency situation are designed to be escalated to a human and to direct patients to emergency services.
  • Educational content used in pilots is drawn from licensed, reviewed medication content sources where applicable.
