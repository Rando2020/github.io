Patient modes

Simple by default. More support when needed.

Simple mode

No jargon

Short plain-language answers focused on what the patient should do next.

More detail

Explain the why

Optional deeper explanations for patients who want to understand medications and benefits.

Caregiver mode

Support with consent

Future caregiver loops for family support, reminders, and care navigation.

Voice future

For low literacy or vision barriers

Voice-first support for patients who cannot or prefer not to type.

Language mode

Preferred language

Designed for multilingual workflows while preserving clinical review boundaries.

Follow-up mode

Close the loop

“Did you get your ride?” “Did you pick up the medication?” “Did the pharmacist call?”

What patients feel

The emotional product is dignity.

Orivo should make patients feel understood without making them feel tested, judged, or forced into a portal.

✓I can ask in my own words.
✓I do not need to know the medical term.
✓I know when a human is involved.
✓I can understand my benefits and medications.
✓I am not alone between visits.
Claims boundary: Orivo is prototype-stage and is not a substitute for emergency care, clinical diagnosis, or provider judgment. Patient-facing guidance must remain plain-language, safe, and routed to humans when clinical review is needed.
