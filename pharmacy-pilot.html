What the pilot targets

The prescriptions you never hear about again.

The pilot is scoped to three operational problems every independent pharmacy recognizes — chosen because they are visible in your own workflow, not because they need a claims feed to prove.

⏸

Abandoned prescriptions

Filled, bagged, waiting on the shelf — then returned to stock. The patient never said why. Orivo's SMS thread gives them a low-friction way to say "I can't afford it" or "I don't understand what it's for" before the return-to-stock date.

↻

Lapsed refills

Patients who quietly stop after a side effect, a confusing instruction, or a cost change. The pilot surfaces the reason in the patient's own words and routes it to your counter as a counseling opportunity, not a mystery.

◍

Undocumented barriers

Transportation, food, language, benefit confusion — the context your pharmacists hear at the counter but rarely have a place to record. Orivo captures it as structured barrier intelligence you can act on and, with consent, share with care partners.

How the 90 days run

A pilot with a beginning, a middle, and a readout.

No open-ended engagement. Each phase has defined work and a defined exit.

Phase 1 Weeks 0–2 · Setup

Consent and enrollment

  • You export a simple patient roster (CSV) — no pharmacy-system integration required
  • Every patient opts in through a documented, plain-language consent flow; STOP works instantly
  • Message templates reviewed and approved by your pharmacist-in-charge, in English and Spanish
  • Escalation rules agreed: what routes to your counter, what routes to 911 guidance
Phase 2 Weeks 2–10 · Live

Live SMS workflow

  • Enrolled patients text questions and concerns in their own words
  • Deterministic triage classifies each message — auditable rules, not a black box
  • Your pharmacists review a prioritized queue; nothing clinical goes out without human approval
  • Weekly 20-minute check-in to tune templates and routing
Phase 3 Weeks 10–13 · Readout

Scorecard and decision

  • You receive the full pilot scorecard — every metric below, measured, with methodology
  • Barrier report: what your patients said was stopping them, categorized
  • Joint decision: continue, expand, or stop — with your data either way
  • Your patient data is yours; export or deletion honored on request

The exchange

What you bring. What you get.

What the pilot needs from you

  • One pharmacist-in-charge as clinical reviewer (queue review fits inside existing counseling workflow)
  • A patient roster export — CSV is enough; we map your columns
  • Willingness to offer opt-in at the counter or by bag insert
  • A weekly 20-minute check-in for the live phase
  • Candor at the readout — including telling us what didn't work

What your pharmacy gets

  • A live triage queue of patient concerns you currently never hear
  • Documented barrier intelligence behind abandoned scripts and lapsed refills
  • A counseling-opportunity feed that supports the adherence measures your quality programs already track
  • The complete scorecard and methodology — yours to keep, whatever you decide
  • Founding-pilot pricing and a voice in what gets built next
Non-negotiables

The rules the pilot runs on.

Human review, alwaysNo AI-generated clinical guidance reaches a patient without pharmacist approval. This is architecture, not policy.
Auditable triageMessage classification uses deterministic rules that can be inspected line by line — chosen deliberately over a black-box model for the pilot phase.
Consent firstTCPA-compliant opt-in, instant STOP handling, and no messaging to patients who haven't agreed. Consent state is logged.
PHI handled properlyNo PHI touches this website. Live pilots run only on infrastructure covered by a business associate agreement, with audit logging.
