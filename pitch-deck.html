How Orivo works

Listen. Translate. Connect.

Three steps. Any phone. Any language. The patient finally has something in their corner, and the care team has context they can act on.

01
Patient speaks naturally
Via SMS, voice, or app. No forms, no portals, no jargon. Symptoms, questions, barriers, and concerns stay in the patient's own words.
02
Aevo translates and enriches
Extracts symptoms, flags urgency, checks medication history, identifies possible ADEs, surfaces SDoH barriers, and loads benefit context.
03
Patient is heard. Team can act.
Patients get plain-language next steps. The care team receives a structured brief with the original patient voice preserved.
The accessibility insight

Everyone else built an app. We didn't.

The patients who need help most are often the same patients every app leaves behind. Orivo meets patients where they already are.

Why we exist
