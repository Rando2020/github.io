New wedge

The pharmacist value gap is a documentation problem.

Pharmacists identify medication harm, resolve adherence barriers, reduce inappropriate medication burden, counsel patients, and coordinate with prescribers. But when the note only says “stopped medication,” the work becomes hard to defend, hard to route, and hard to reimburse.
