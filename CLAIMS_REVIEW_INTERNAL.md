# Internal Claims Review for Orivo Health

This file is for founder/internal use. Do not link it from the public site.

## Public claims rule

Use sources to support the healthcare problem and product rationale. Do not imply Orivo has achieved outcomes until pilot data exists.

## Safer public language

- Orivo is designed to support adherence workflows tied to PDC-tracked medication classes.
- Orivo captures patient-reported medication concerns and routes potential safety signals to pharmacists or care teams for review.
- Orivo is prototype-stage and designed for future HIPAA-aligned deployment.
- Orivo uses public evidence to support the problem space, not Orivo-specific outcomes.

## Avoid until validated

- Orivo improves PDC.
- Orivo reduces ADEs.
- Orivo improves Star Ratings.
- Orivo reduces hospitalizations.
- Orivo is HIPAA compliant in production.
- Orivo supports every insurance plan.
- Orivo supports every country or language operationally.
