Evolution map

The disciplined path to something huge.

H0

Prototype credibility

Clickable demos, evidence registry, claims boundary, sector pages, and safety-first messaging. Proof needed: users understand the concept in under 60 seconds.

H1

Pharmacy wedge

Aevo Relay + Orivo Brief for medication confusion, side effects, refill issues, and adherence barriers. Proof needed: pharmacist-reviewed interventions and documented resolution loops.

H2

Medication safety layer

Aevo Rx + Vera validation for med reconciliation, side-effect routing, and safe escalation. Proof needed: routing accuracy, pharmacist trust, and intervention documentation quality.

H3

Payer intelligence

Orivo Intel surfaces ride needs, affordability issues, benefit confusion, and adherence risk patterns. Proof needed: de-identified trends that quality and population health teams actually use.

H4

Provider integration

EHR-ready notes, FHIR-aligned integration targets, and role-based routing. Proof needed: lower context-gathering burden without increasing provider noise.

H5

National patient layer

SMS, voice, caregiver mode, multilingual workflows, and payer/provider/pharmacy interoperability. Proof needed: scalable engagement, trust, compliance maturity, and measurable resolution.

H6

Global access layer

Low-bandwidth SMS/voice health navigation for community health, NGOs, governments, and underserved regions. Proof needed: local pilots, regulatory localization, and multilingual validation.
